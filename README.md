## Hi there 👋

I’m a postdoctoral research fellow at the [Barcelona Institute for Global Health](https://www.isglobal.org/).

My current research focuses on the development of statistical microsimulation and forecasting models to project the health effects of changes in Official Development Assistance (ODA) in low- and middle-income countries. 

My other research interests include the development of statistical models for infectious disease epidemiology, specifically HIV and SARS-CoV-2.

In my PhD I investigated the application of [multi-state models to estimate infectious disease burden](https://doi.org/10.17863/CAM.112581). You can read some excerpts in [this blog series](https://www.pkirwan.com/#blog), or check out the [cd4backcalc R package](https://pkirwan.github.io/cd4backcalc/).

Previously at the [MRC Biostatistics Unit at the University of Cambridge](https://www.medschl.cam.ac.uk/institutions/mrc-bsu) and [Public Health England](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/hiv-surveillance-data-and-management).
